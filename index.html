The scope of work

Ten ways we move dirt
& open up land.

Every job below runs off our own equipment: skid steer with mulching head, mini excavator and the attachments to match. Pick one line item or hand us the whole project.

01

Stump Grinding

Full stump removal down below grade so the ground is ready to seed, pave or build over, with no leftover root mass to deal with later.

02

Excavation

Foundation digs, footers and general dirt moving, graded to spec and ready for the next trade.

03

Driveways

New driveway cuts, gravel base work and driveway reconditioning, crowned and ditched so water sheds off instead of pooling on your lane.

04

Waterlines

Trenching and installation for new water service runs, backfilled and compacted to the line.

05

Culverts

Culvert pipe installed and sized for the drainage it needs to carry, driveway crossings included.

06

Site Prep

Clearing, grading and rough leveling to get a build site, pad or lot to a ready-to-construct state.

07

Drainage

French drains, swales and grading fixes for yards and fields that won't dry out on their own.

08

Land Clearing

Brush, timber and overgrowth cleared for pasture, building sites or reclaiming ground that's grown over.

09

Forestry Mulching

Mulching head turns brush and small timber into ground cover in one pass: one trip in, no burn piles, no hauling.

10

Snow & Lane Clearing

Driveway and lane plowing to keep access open through the winter, pushed clear down to gravel.
